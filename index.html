About

Two things sit side by side in my work: the vigilance of keeping infrastructure upright, and the quiet satisfaction of automating away a task so it never has to be done by hand again.

I work the Command Center floor — Windows, Network, Cloud, SRE/Unix, Cybersecurity, and Storage all crossing my desk — escalating incidents and coordinating with clients when something needs a human voice, fast. Outside the floor, I build: SharePoint sites for Classic and Modern experience, Power Apps forms and canvas apps, Power Automate flows, SPFx customizations, and exporter tools that turn raw monitoring data into dashboards people actually look at.

I passed the PhilNITS Fundamental Information Technology Engineer exam and JLPT N4 during training, and I've supported cross-functional technical and Japan-facing teams since. I like problems with a clear before-and-after: manual today, automatic tomorrow.
